    Where the defaults come from
    • Mortgage rate 7.25%: the 30-year average was 7.28% on Nov 19, 2026 (Freddie Mac).
    • Child, by age: Massachusetts childcare runs about $2,200 to $2,800 a month for infants, $1,800 to $2,400 for toddlers, $1,400 to $2,000 for preschool and about $1,400 for school-age care (Wonderschool, DaycareCalc). Costs do not simply fall with age: USDA found total spending per child roughly flat, about $12,350 to $13,900 a year in 2015 dollars, a little lower for ages 0 to 2 and higher for teens (USDA). LendingTree puts the Massachusetts 18-year total near $274,000 to $282,000, with about $34,000 a year for a small child, mostly day care (LendingTree). Our defaults add up to about $366,000 per child over 18 years (plus the one-time cost), above those averages because the Boston area and a higher income cost more.
    • Closing costs 2.5%: Massachusetts estimates range from about 1.3% to 2.4% (Rocket Mortgage, Bankrate).
    • Insurance $400/mo: state averages are only about $1,600 to $2,100 a year for $250K to $400K of cover, but a $1M-dwelling policy was quoted near $480 a month (MoneyGeek).
    • Paid leave: Massachusetts pays up to 12 weeks for bonding, capped at $1,230.39 a week in 2026 (Mass.gov), so higher earners lose pay unless an employer tops it up.
    • Our own estimates, not sourced: the split of child costs from age 3 to 18, extra utilities, upkeep, the one-time cost per child, property tax near $12 per $1,000, and 3% yearly home appreciation. Mortgage insurance (0.5% of the loan a year) is added automatically when you put down less than 20%.

    Simplified model for planning, not advice. Savings grow at the return you set; a house is bought at today's price grown by appreciation; housing cost = mortgage + property tax + insurance + upkeep + extra utilities (plus mortgage insurance under 20% down), replacing your current rent. Each child costs a set amount per month at each age band (0 to 2, 3 to 4, 5 to 12, 13 to 18). Retirement and other assets grow at the same return with no new contributions.
